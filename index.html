Privacy policy

Support

Kinglet is a collection of small games for iPhone that work completely offline. Got a question, found a bug or have an idea for a new game? Write to hellokinglet@gmail.com.

Questions

Where are my records and levels saved?
Only on your phone. If you delete the app they are gone, and they do not move to another phone.

How do I reset my records?
Tap the gear at the top right of the game list and choose Reset records.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?
Tap the gear at the top right of the game list.

Do I need a connection?
No. Everything in the app works in airplane mode.
