Privacy policy

Kinglet

Support

Kinglet collects no personal data

The app has no accounts and no ads. The games work without an internet connection.

Records, levels, statistics and settings are stored only on your phone. If you delete the app, they are gone.

Anonymous statistics

To see which games are played and how they can be made better, the app sends anonymous statistics when it has a connection: which games are started, how rounds end and how long they last, and the app's version and language.

The statistics go to TelemetryDeck, a service in Germany, in the EU. They are linked only to a random number made on your phone, never to your name, your Apple Account, your device or where you are. They are not used for advertising or tracking across apps, and they are never sold.

You can turn them off at any time in the app, under Settings, Share Anonymous Statistics. Nothing is sent after that.

Purchases and Game Center

The Classics pack is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment, and I do not see who you are or how you paid.

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best scores in the score games go to Apple's leaderboards, where other players can see them with your Game Center name. Apple's privacy policy applies to Game Center.

Apple's statistics

If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may show me combined statistics, such as how many people downloaded the app and whether it crashed. These statistics cannot be linked to you.
